Roads and paths: OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), each used only by those allowed on it; driveways, car parks and private roads are left out. By car: every road a car can use, A roads up to 34 mph, B roads 27, C roads 25, other lanes 23, estate roads 18, green lanes 12, each capped at 85% of the posted limit, plus 3 seconds for every junction passed; one-way streets respected; no traffic, lights or queues for the tunnel. The 20 mph island cuts every higher limit to 20 mph, at the same 85%. Road closures shut a stretch of road between two junctions, both ways, to through traffic; a journey may still start along a closed stretch, as someone living there would. The roadworks closures load in your browser from the Government of Jersey's roadworks map (roadworks.gov.je): works marked Road Closure that are approved or in progress on the chosen day, whatever their hours, matched to the roads they lie on; signed diversions are drawn for reference. Check the official map before you travel. By bike: 16 km/h on the flat (11 on rough tracks), slower uphill and up to 30 km/h down, plus 2 seconds a junction; bikes are pushed along footways and pedestrian streets and kept off steps; one-way streets respected unless bikes are let through. On foot: Tobler's hiking pace, 5 km/h on the flat, on every road, path and set of steps. Heights: Mapzen terrain tiles (EU-DEM, produced using Copernicus data and information funded by the European Union, and SRTM). People: each parish's 2021 census population (Statistics Jersey) spread over its homes by floor area, from OpenStreetMap buildings. Land: 50 m squares, each given to its nearest junction. Adapted from Doug Belshaw's All Roads Lead To (UK), after Ethan Mollick's US version and “All Roads Lead to Richmond” by Arterial Mapping. Source code (GPL-3.0).
